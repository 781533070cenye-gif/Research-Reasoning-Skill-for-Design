# Research Reasoning Skill for Design

A reusable research-reasoning skill designed for design research, HCI, and interdisciplinary academic research.

It supports researchers in moving from:
**research material → evidence → claims → theoretical framing → paper structure**

## What this skill does

This skill helps with:

- identifying the real research question behind fragmented ideas
- separating evidence, interpretation, and speculation
- building claim–evidence relationships
- integrating qualitative and quantitative findings
- avoiding premature theorization
- refining contributions for HCI and design research
- maintaining consistency across Introduction, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion
