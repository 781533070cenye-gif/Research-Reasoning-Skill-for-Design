## Finding Card

- **Finding:** One substantive sentence.
- **Evidence:** Direct analyses or materials.
- **Pattern:** Structure, magnitude, distribution, sequence, or exception.
- **Interpretation:** Supported explanation, explicitly labeled.
- **Boundary:** What the evidence does not establish.
- **Role:** Why this finding must remain in the main paper.
